About Elizabeth

Passionate About Science and Helping Students Progress

Elizabeth is currently in her second year studying Biochemistry at the University of Sheffield. She is passionate about science and enjoys helping students expand their knowledge and become more confident with scientific ideas.

Her lessons are encouraging and friendly. Elizabeth looks for each student's existing academic strengths and helps them use those strengths effectively, while also working through areas they may find more challenging.

She can support students with understanding difficult content, improving revision methods and developing a stronger approach to answering exam questions.

Elizabeth tutors Biology, Chemistry and Combined Science at KS3 and GCSE. At A-Level, she offers tuition specifically in Biology and Chemistry.
