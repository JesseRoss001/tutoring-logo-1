Individual learning needs

Patient, adaptable support for students who learn differently

Mandy has experience supporting children and young people with special educational needs, including learners with autism and ADHD.

She understands that students may need different explanations, lesson structures, pacing or levels of repetition to make learning accessible and manageable.

Her approach focuses on understanding the learner as an individual and creating lessons that help them feel comfortable, capable and increasingly independent.

Personalised support may include

  • Adjusting lesson pace and structure to the individual learner
  • Explaining concepts in different ways when something does not immediately make sense
  • Breaking larger tasks into smaller and more manageable stages
  • Building confidence through achievable and visible progress
  • Revisiting key knowledge when additional reinforcement is useful
