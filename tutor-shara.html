About Shara

Qualified teaching experience combined with personalised tuition

Clear explanations, carefully structured learning and targeted practice designed around the individual student.

Shara is a qualified Science teacher who provides one-to-one and small-group tuition both online and face-to-face. He supports students across KS3, GCSE and A-Level Biology, Chemistry and Physics, tailoring lessons to individual abilities, learning needs and areas of difficulty.

His teaching focuses on making complex Science topics more accessible, strengthening subject knowledge and helping students develop the confidence to apply what they have learned.

Alongside subject understanding, Shara places strong emphasis on exam technique, structured learning and targeted exam practice. He aims to identify gaps early, explain difficult concepts clearly and monitor progress as lessons develop.

Exam technique

Understanding the Science is only part of the task

Students can understand a topic in lessons but still find it difficult to convert that knowledge into marks in an examination.

Shara uses targeted exam practice to help students recognise what a question is asking, choose the relevant knowledge, structure their response and manage their time more effectively.

Building confidence through practice

Lessons can combine direct teaching, worked examples, supported practice and independent exam-style questions.

This allows Shara to see not only whether the student remembers the Science, but whether they can actually apply it.
