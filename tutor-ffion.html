About Ffion

Experienced support for vocational qualifications and assessments

Ffion is an experienced tutor specialising in Health & Social Care and Child Development. She has experience teaching and supporting students from Entry Level through to Level 3, including coursework, assignments, examinations and subject knowledge.

She also has experience across a broader range of vocational and academic subjects including Travel and Tourism, Sociology, Criminology, Welsh Baccalaureate and Entry Level and Level 1 Introduction to Vocational Studies.

Ffion's lessons are friendly, patient and approachable. She adapts her teaching around each learner and aims to make difficult content easier to understand while helping students become more confident and independent.

Moderator & examiner experience

Alongside teaching and tutoring, Ffion works as a moderator and examiner. This gives her a strong understanding of what assessors expect from students and how work is judged, helping learners understand how to improve coursework, assignments and examination responses.
