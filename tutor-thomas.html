About Thomas

Strong subject knowledge combined with engaging teaching

Thomas is highly proficient in Maths and Physics and is currently studying Physics at Durham University. His academic background gives him a strong understanding of the ideas and methods required for GCSE success.

He is ready to teach students regardless of their current confidence or skill level. Lessons can provide an extra helping hand with difficult topics from school or offer further challenge to students who want to explore beyond the standard curriculum.

Thomas’s teaching is specific, example-led and based around practice. This gives students opportunities to identify problems in their understanding and work through them clearly.

His main priorities are student engagement and strong subject knowledge, helping learners understand not only what method to use, but why it works.
