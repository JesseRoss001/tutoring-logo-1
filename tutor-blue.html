About Blue

Helping students understand English, not just memorise it

Blue is a second-year English Literature student at the University of Bristol and achieved an A* at A-Level, alongside Grade 9s in both GCSE English Language and English Literature.

Having recently been through the GCSE and A-Level exam process, Blue understands the demands students face and focuses on practical approaches that strengthen analysis, writing, exam technique and confidence.

Lessons balance patience with clear expectations. Blue believes that developing a friendly and respectful relationship with students helps them become more engaged, attentive and willing to complete work independently between sessions.

“Whether a student wants to build confidence, sharpen their exam technique or simply enjoy English more, the approach should be adapted to how that individual learns best.”

Individual learning

Different students need different approaches

Blue's awareness of different learning styles began long before tutoring. As one of three triplets sitting the same examinations at the same time, Blue saw first-hand that students can respond very differently to the same material and teaching method.

That experience now informs Blue's tutoring. Rather than forcing every student through one fixed method, lessons can be adapted around the learner's strengths, confidence, pace and preferred way of approaching a task.

One method does not fit everyone

Even students of the same age, studying the same course and sitting the same exam may need very different explanations. Blue uses this understanding to identify what works best for the individual student.
