About Shannon

Personalised Teaching That Builds Confidence

Shannon is a qualified and experienced teacher with a passion for helping children, young people and adult learners reach their potential.

English is her main specialism, but she also has experience tutoring Welsh and Modern Foreign Languages, with Spanish being her strongest Modern Foreign Language.

Throughout her teaching career, Shannon has worked with learners of different ages, abilities and backgrounds, from students who require additional support to high-attaining learners aiming to stretch themselves further.

Her lessons are adapted around the individual student, using clear explanations, targeted feedback and personalised strategies to help them develop greater academic confidence and independence.
