About Sadia

Experienced Science teaching with a friendly, supportive approach

Sadia is a friendly, experienced and dedicated Science tutor with a strong academic background and a particular specialism in Chemistry.

Alongside her school teaching, Sadia has spent ten years tutoring students privately. Her experience includes GCSE Science and A-Level Chemistry tuition, with much of her current tuition delivered online.
