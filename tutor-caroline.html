About Caroline

Experienced, qualified teaching with detailed examiner knowledge

Caroline is a fully qualified secondary school teacher specialising in Science and Maths. She has over 18 years of teaching and tutoring experience within schools and online.

Her experience includes mainstream education and special educational needs teaching. She has successfully supported pupils preparing for 11 Plus and 13 Plus entrance examinations, GCSE Maths and Science, and A-Level Biology and Chemistry.

Caroline has a proven track record of helping pupils improve their confidence, understanding and exam performance. Students have frequently achieved the grades they were working towards, with some achieving higher results than originally expected.

She is patient, friendly, encouraging and reliable, and places considerable importance on adapting lessons to the individual pupil.

GCSE and A-Level examiner insight

Caroline’s examining experience allows her to explain what examiners are looking for within pupils’ answers and where marks are commonly gained or lost.

Pupils learn how to interpret command words, include the correct scientific detail and structure answers clearly enough to meet the requirements of the mark scheme.

  • Understanding command words and question requirements
  • Applying knowledge to unfamiliar questions
  • Developing complete, mark-focused answers
  • Learning from past-paper feedback
  • Improving confidence under exam conditions
