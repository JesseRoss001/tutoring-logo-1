01 About Grace

Patient support with a clear purpose

Grace's lessons are patient, supportive and adapted to each student's needs. She is happy to work at the student's pace, giving them the time they need to develop a secure understanding before moving on.

A student may need to close gaps from earlier learning, strengthen one particular topic, prepare for examinations or move ahead of their current class. Grace creates a structured plan around that individual goal.

She is also particularly good at recognising whether a difficulty is caused by the Maths itself or by the way a student is approaching exam questions. That helps lessons focus on the area that is most likely to make a difference.

Grace's aim is for students to leave each session feeling more secure in their understanding and increasingly confident in their ability to solve problems independently.

Finding the Real Problem

Is It a Topic Problem or an Exam Question Problem?

Grace is good at distinguishing between these two different issues, helping lesson time focus on what the student actually needs.

Topic Understanding

Sometimes the underlying mathematical idea itself is not yet secure.

  • Revisit the concept clearly
  • Break the method into manageable steps
  • Strengthen understanding before harder work
VS

Exam Question Technique

Other students know the Maths but struggle when the same skill appears in an unfamiliar examination question.

  • Interpret what the question is asking
  • Identify the correct method
  • Communicate working more effectively
Lesson Approach

A Clear Path Towards Progress

Grace keeps lessons purposeful by identifying what needs attention, working through it carefully and helping students become less reliant on support over time.

1

Identify

Find the student's current strengths, difficulties and goals.

2

Focus

Target the topic or question skill that is most useful to work on.

3

Practise

Work through problems at an appropriate pace until understanding becomes more secure.

4

Build Independence

Gradually help the student solve problems with less guidance and greater confidence.

The aim is for students to leave each lesson more secure in their understanding and more confident solving problems independently.

Grace's teaching is patient and supportive, but the long-term goal is to help students rely increasingly on their own mathematical understanding.
