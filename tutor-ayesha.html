About Ayesha

Making difficult subjects feel clearer and more manageable

Ayesha is a Master of Science graduate and experienced private tutor who is passionate about the subjects she teaches. Her aim is not simply to help students prepare for exams, but to make learning engaging enough that students begin to enjoy and understand the subject.

Lessons are interactive, supportive and adapted to the individual student. Ayesha encourages learners to ask questions, explore ideas and make mistakes without feeling uncomfortable or rushed.

Rather than overwhelming students with too much information at once, she breaks larger concepts into manageable sections and works through them at a pace that allows understanding to become secure.

Clear explanations

One of Ayesha's key strengths is explaining complex concepts in straightforward language, supported by diagrams, examples and real-life applications where these help the student understand more effectively.

Teaching approach

Breaking difficult ideas into manageable steps

Ayesha believes students learn more effectively when they are given time to understand each stage of a concept rather than being presented with too much information at once.

Lessons therefore move progressively from explanation to understanding, then into application and exam practice.

01

Simplify

Break complex topics into smaller sections that are easier to understand and remember.

02

Visualise

Use diagrams and real-life examples where these make an abstract idea easier to understand.

03

Check understanding

Encourage questions and active discussion to check that each concept has genuinely become clear.

04

Apply

Move into questions and past-paper practice so the student can use their knowledge independently.

Learning environment

A positive space where students can ask questions

Ayesha aims to create lessons where students feel comfortable exploring ideas, making mistakes and asking questions without worrying about getting everything right immediately.

This supportive environment allows students to develop confidence while still working towards clear academic goals and measurable progress.

Comfortable asking questions Students are encouraged to speak up whenever something is unclear.
Individual pace Topics are worked through at a pace suited to the learner.
Active listening Lessons respond to what the student understands rather than following a rigid plan.
Enjoyable learning Sessions aim to make learning more engaging while maintaining a clear academic purpose.
Goal-oriented lessons

Clear and achievable goals help Ayesha identify what is working for each student and where lessons need to adapt.

Step 2

Step 3

Practise

Step 4

Adapt
