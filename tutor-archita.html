About Archita

Supportive tuition designed around the individual learner

Archita is currently in her second year of a BSc Psychology with a Professional Placement Year at King's College London. Alongside university, she has two years of tutoring experience supporting students across Primary, KS3, GCSE and A-Level.

Her tutoring experience covers a broad range of subjects, including Maths, English, Biology, Combined Science, Psychology and Sociology. She supports students with understanding difficult topics, preparing for assessments and becoming more confident in their academic abilities.

Archita's Psychology background gives her a strong interest in how people learn, what motivates students and why different approaches can work better for different learners.

She therefore adapts lessons around each student's individual needs, ability, learning style and goals. Her aim is not only to improve academic understanding, but also to help learners become increasingly confident and independent.

Psychology & learning

Understanding how different students learn

As a Psychology student at King's College London, Archita brings both subject knowledge and a growing academic understanding of learning, motivation and the differences between individual learners into her tutoring.

She combines this with practical tutoring experience to adjust explanations, lesson pace and activities depending on what helps the student understand most effectively.

Individual learning needs

Lessons are adapted around the learner rather than expecting every student to respond to the same teaching method.

Motivation

Archita aims to make lessons engaging so students can recognise their progress and feel motivated to keep improving.

Independence

Students are encouraged to become increasingly confident solving problems and approaching work independently.

Lesson approach

From difficulty to confidence

Lessons are structured around identifying what the learner needs, making difficult ideas manageable and helping them apply their understanding independently.

01

Identify

Establish the student's current understanding, difficulties and academic goals.

02

Explain

Break complex topics into clear and manageable explanations.

03

Practise

Apply learning through suitable questions and assessment-focused practice.

04

Build confidence

Help the learner become increasingly independent and secure in their understanding.

Archita's aim is simple: to help every student feel more confident in what they can achieve.

Confidence first

A supportive environment where mistakes are part of learning

Archita aims to create lessons where students feel comfortable admitting when they do not understand something, asking questions and attempting challenging work without worrying about making mistakes.

Her focus is not only on improving subject knowledge, but also helping students become more confident in themselves and their ability to tackle unfamiliar problems.

Beyond academics

Creativity, discipline and perseverance

Archita has competed in dance, karate, table tennis, debating and elocution. These experiences have helped her develop qualities that she also brings into her tutoring.

Patience

Students can work through difficult topics at an appropriate pace without feeling rushed.

Adaptability

Teaching methods can change depending on the student's needs, ability and response to an explanation.

Communication

Organisation

Lessons are prepared with the student's current goals in mind so tutoring remains purposeful and focused.
