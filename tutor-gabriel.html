Lesson structure

A 40/60 balance between explanation and active student practice

Gabriel's usual lesson structure gives students enough time to understand new ideas while ensuring that the majority of the session involves actively applying their knowledge.

40%

Teaching & Explanation

Approximately 40% of a lesson is dedicated to explaining concepts, introducing new topics, reviewing important knowledge and demonstrating methods for solving problems.

Gabriel breaks complex ideas into smaller, manageable steps and uses worked examples to explain the reasoning behind each method.

60%

Student-Led Practice & Application

The remaining 60% gives students opportunities to apply their knowledge independently while Gabriel provides guidance and support when needed.

Practice can include exam-style questions, problem-solving, discussing different approaches and identifying areas that need further improvement.

Teaching approach

Patient support with plenty of opportunity to practise

Gabriel recognises that every student learns differently and adapts the balance of each lesson to suit their confidence, understanding and individual learning needs.

Patient and approachable

Students are given time to work through difficult ideas without feeling pressured or embarrassed about making mistakes.

Active student practice

Students are encouraged to attempt questions themselves so Gabriel can identify gaps in understanding and provide targeted feedback.

Exam familiarity

Regular practice helps students become more comfortable with the different ways their knowledge may be tested in GCSE examinations.

Building independence

Gabriel aims to help students become more confident solving problems and applying their knowledge without relying constantly on support.
