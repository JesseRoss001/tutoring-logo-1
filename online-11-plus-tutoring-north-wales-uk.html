Understanding the assessment

What is the 11 Plus exam?

The 11 Plus, also called the Eleven Plus, is an entrance assessment used by some grammar schools and selective independent schools. The format is not identical everywhere, so families should always check the admissions information for each target school.

What can the test include?

Depending on the school or test provider, an 11 Plus examination may include maths, English, verbal reasoning, non-verbal reasoning, comprehension, vocabulary and timed multiple-choice questions.

Does every school use the same paper?

No. Different schools and regions can use different test providers, question styles, timings and admissions arrangements. Our tuition is tailored once the target schools are known.

When should preparation begin?

The right starting point depends on your child’s current level, test date and confidence. Steady preparation over time is usually more constructive than intense last-minute pressure.

Is tutoring suitable for every child?

Tutoring can help children who need extension, targeted support, exam familiarity or greater confidence. We will be honest about the support that appears most appropriate for your child.
