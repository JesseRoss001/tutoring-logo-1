About Elan

Science tutoring built around understanding rather than last-minute memorisation

Elan is a final-year Biomedical Science student at Cardiff University and hopes to continue into Medicine after completing the degree.

Science has always been a strong area of interest, particularly human biology, and Elan wants to pass that enthusiasm on to students who may currently be finding Science challenging.

The main focus of Elan's tutoring is understanding. Rather than encouraging students simply to memorise information immediately before an exam, lessons aim to help them understand why concepts work and how different ideas connect.

Once the underlying Science makes sense, retaining and recalling the information can become more natural and students are better placed to apply their knowledge to unfamiliar questions.

Teaching approach

Finding the explanation that makes the topic finally click

Elan recognises that the same explanation will not work for every student and is prepared to approach difficult topics in different ways.

Understanding first

The priority is making sure students understand the underlying Science before asking them to memorise large amounts of information.

Different ways of explaining

If one explanation does not work, Elan can take a different approach and break the topic down in another way.

Supportive communication

Lessons aim to give students space to ask questions, identify what they do not understand and discuss difficult ideas openly.

More natural recall

Once students understand the reasoning behind a concept, memorising key facts and applying knowledge can become much easier.
