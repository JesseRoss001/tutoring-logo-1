About Tomos

Strong Maths Knowledge with a Friendly Approach

Tomos is originally from Carmarthen in West Wales and is currently in his final year studying Mathematics at Cardiff University.

He achieved A* grades in Mathematics at both GCSE and A-Level and is fluent in both Welsh and English.

Tomos is relatively new to tutoring and has recently started providing one-to-one online Maths lessons for GCSE and A-Level students. He brings strong mathematical knowledge, patience and an approachable style to his sessions.

His aim is to create an environment where students feel comfortable asking questions, working through difficult ideas and developing greater confidence in their own ability.
