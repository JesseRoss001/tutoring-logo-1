About Bevan

Strong recent subject knowledge with a supportive approach to learning

Bevan is currently a third-year Medical Student at Cardiff University and achieved A* grades in Biology, Chemistry and Mathematics at A-Level.

He primarily offers support with WJEC A-Level Biology and Chemistry, while also tutoring A-Level Mathematics and GCSE Biology, Chemistry and Mathematics.

As someone who has recently completed his own A-Levels, Bevan understands the pressure students can experience when preparing for important examinations. His aim is to make lessons supportive and approachable so students feel comfortable asking questions and learning from mistakes.

Bevan is new to tutoring and brings recent first-hand experience of succeeding in these subjects alongside the deeper scientific understanding he continues to develop through his medical studies.
