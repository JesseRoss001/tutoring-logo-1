About Siân

Experienced, Supportive and Adaptable Teaching

Siân has worked with learners across a wide range of ages and abilities throughout a career spanning more than 30 years in education. She enjoys adapting her teaching to suit the individual rather than expecting every learner to work in the same way.

Her aim is to make lessons friendly, engaging and supportive while helping learners develop both their skills and their confidence. Clear explanations, questioning, encouragement and praise are central to the way she teaches.

Siân is a fluent Welsh speaker and is confident teaching through both Welsh and English. Alongside Primary tuition, she can support Welsh language learners from childhood through adulthood.

More than three decades in education

Siân's experience covers Early Years, Primary classrooms, one-to-one support, Foundation Phase coordination, online teaching and Welsh-medium supply teaching. This broad background means she is comfortable adapting to different learners, abilities and learning environments.

Teaching Style

Building Confidence Step by Step

Siân describes her main strengths as her ability to build a positive rapport with learners, her patience and her flexibility.

She is encouraging and supportive while maintaining clear expectations. When a learner finds something difficult, she breaks the learning into manageable steps and tries different approaches until it becomes clearer.

She particularly enjoys helping learners recognise their own progress and grow in confidence.

Build Rapport

Create a friendly and supportive environment where the learner feels comfortable asking questions.

Adapt the Lesson

Adjust explanations, resources and pace around the individual learner.

Break Learning Down

Turn difficult skills into smaller, manageable steps that learners can tackle confidently.

Recognise Progress

Use questioning, praise and encouragement to help learners recognise how far they have come.
