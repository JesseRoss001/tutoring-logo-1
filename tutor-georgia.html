Georgia is currently in her third year studying Medicine at Cardiff University and has a strong academic background.

She achieved A*AA at A-Level, including an A* in Biology.

Georgia's Approach

Supportive Without Doing the Thinking for Them

Georgia encourages students to work through problems independently rather than simply giving them the answers.

Her aim is to help students understand both the material and the process they can use when they meet similar questions on their own.
