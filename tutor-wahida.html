About Wahida

Experienced English teaching supported by examiner insight

Wahida is a secondary English teacher with over 13 years of experience teaching KS3 and KS4, alongside several years of private tutoring.

She delivers tailored one-to-one and small-group lessons online and in person, specialising in GCSE English Language and Literature.

As an AQA and Edexcel examiner, Wahida has a strong understanding of exam requirements and uses this knowledge to help students improve their written responses, exam technique and confidence.

Her lessons are engaging, well structured and adapted to the individual student, whether they need to strengthen their foundations, improve an existing grade or work towards the highest results.

Examiner expertise

Focused preparation based on what examiners are looking for

Wahida’s examining experience gives her valuable insight into assessment objectives, mark schemes and the qualities that distinguish stronger responses.

She uses this knowledge to provide specific feedback and help students understand how to improve their analysis, structure, evidence and written expression.

Students are supported in applying their knowledge effectively under exam conditions rather than relying only on memorised content.

Exam preparation can include

  • Understanding assessment objectives and mark schemes
  • Planning clear and relevant written responses
  • Improving language and literature analysis
  • Developing effective use of quotations and evidence
  • Targeted feedback on exam-style work
