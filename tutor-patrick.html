About Patrick

Experienced English teaching with a flexible, student-centred approach

Patrick is currently studying for a Secondary English PGCE at the University of Manchester and has three years of experience teaching English Language.

He previously taught English as a second language in Spain and Italy, supporting students with exam preparation, structured writing, reading comprehension, listening and grammatical accuracy.

Patrick's approach is organised, adaptable and built around the needs of the individual student. He believes that strong rapport makes lessons more productive and helps students feel comfortable asking questions and engaging fully with the work.
