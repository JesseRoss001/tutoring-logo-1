About Jonathan

Experienced teaching built around confidence, rapport and progress

Jonathan graduated from the University of Hull in 2000 with a 2:1 BA (Hons) degree in Business Management. He has since developed extensive experience teaching and tutoring students across different ages, academic levels and educational backgrounds.

He has a strong track record of supporting students studying English Language and English Literature at GCSE and A-Level, with students going on to improve their grades after working with him.

Jonathan also teaches Business and Economics and has extensive experience with A-Level Business Studies. In addition, he has many years of experience teaching A-Level History and Geography across exam boards.

After many years in education, Jonathan remains passionate about helping students reach their potential. His lessons aim to be productive, professional and academically useful while also remaining enjoyable and approachable.
