University maths tutoring: quick answers

Looking for a university maths tutor? Start here.

These are the questions students most often need answered before choosing university mathematics tuition.

Can you find me a tutor for my exact university maths module?

Yes, where we have a suitable tutor available. Send us your module title, syllabus or current topics. We use that information to identify a maths tutor whose teaching level and subject knowledge are appropriate for what you are studying.

Do I need a university maths tutor near me?

No. Our university maths tuition is online, so students searching for a university maths tutor near them can access one-to-one support from North Wales Tutoring without being limited by local tutor availability.

Which university maths topics can you support?

A broad range. Depending on tutor availability, this can include calculus, linear algebra, differential equations, probability, statistics, numerical methods, discrete maths, real analysis, pure maths, applied maths and engineering mathematics.

Can lessons prepare me for university maths exams?

Yes. Tuition can focus on understanding difficult topics, working through exam-style questions, reviewing errors, improving mathematical notation and creating a revision plan around your assessment dates.
