About May

Academic support built around confidence, curiosity and independence

May believes that confidence is one of the foundations of academic achievement. She takes time to understand each learner's goals, learning style and specific challenges so that tuition can be shaped around the individual rather than delivered through a fixed approach.

Her experience spans more than 14 years across further and higher education, professional coaching and technology client leadership. This interdisciplinary background allows May to connect academic ideas with real-world examples and help students see relationships between subjects.

May currently delivers Business and Management courses at Levels 4 to 7 at BPP and also teaches through Hammersmith and Fulham Adult Learning and Skills Service. She previously worked as Deputy Course Director, Module Leader and Lecturer in Business and Management at Coventry University London, teaching international cohorts across Levels 4 to 7.

Her wider academic experience has included study and development across Oxford, Cambridge, UCL and Central Saint Martins, alongside professional experience spanning business, heritage and the arts.

University examiner perspective

May has served as an External Examiner at the University of Suffolk and the University of Gloucestershire, as well as an External Panel Member at Oxford Brookes University.

This gives her direct experience of university-level assessment standards and the differences between work that demonstrates basic knowledge and work that shows stronger analysis, evaluation and academic judgement.

She uses this assessment experience to help students communicate their ideas more clearly and understand how to strengthen the quality of their written work.

University & postgraduate support

Helping students move beyond knowing the content

At university level, knowing the subject is only one part of producing strong academic work. Students also need to develop argument, critical thinking, evidence use, structure and an understanding of what the assessment is actually asking them to do.

May's experience as a lecturer, module leader and university External Examiner allows her to support students with these wider academic skills alongside their subject knowledge.

She also supports learners working on higher-level research and academic writing, including undergraduate and postgraduate projects and students progressing towards academic publication.

Academic coaching

Building skills students can use beyond a single exam or assignment

May brings a coaching mindset into tuition, helping students strengthen not only subject knowledge but also the confidence, resilience and independent thinking they need to manage their own learning.

Understand the learner

May starts by understanding the student's goals, current challenges, learning style and what is holding back their progress.

Create direction

Structured coaching frameworks such as GROW, CLEAR and OSKAR can be used to turn broad goals into clearer, manageable actions.

Make learning memorable

May uses connections and storytelling to bring knowledge to life, helping students understand why ideas matter rather than simply memorising information.

Build independence

Students are encouraged to take increasing ownership of their work, think critically and develop strategies they can continue using independently.
