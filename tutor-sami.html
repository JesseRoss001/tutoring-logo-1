About Sami

Friendly GCSE Science tuition built around each student's individual needs

Sami is a passionate and supportive one-to-one Science tutor who works collaboratively with students to identify gaps in their knowledge and decide where lessons should focus.

He breaks difficult Science topics into clear, manageable explanations, helping students build a stronger understanding before moving on to more challenging questions and examination practice.

Having completed GCSE and A-Level examinations himself, Sami also draws on his own academic experience to help students with effective memorisation techniques, study habits and revision strategies.

His aim is to create a supportive learning environment where students feel comfortable asking questions, attempting difficult tasks and developing the confidence to work more independently.
