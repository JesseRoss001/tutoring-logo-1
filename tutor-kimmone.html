About Kimmone

Clear explanations combined with focused academic and exam support

Kimmone has more than two years of one-to-one tutoring experience and works with students across a wide range of ages and academic levels.

His lessons focus on understanding what each learner needs, identifying areas that are holding them back and then adapting teaching accordingly.

He has supported students who were previously struggling or failing subjects and helped them progress towards passes and strong overall grades, including A*, A and B outcomes.

Alongside school-level tutoring, Kimmone also supports university students studying quantitative modules, statistics, probability, Economics and related Mathematics.
