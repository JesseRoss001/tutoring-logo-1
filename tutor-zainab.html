About Zainab

Making difficult Science and Maths easier to understand

Zainab is currently studying Medicine at Bangor University and has more than three years of tutoring experience, with over 700 hours spent supporting students.

Her main tutoring experience is across Biology, Chemistry and Maths, where she works with students of different abilities and adapts lessons according to their individual needs. She also provides support in Physics and Combined Science.

Zainab focuses on breaking complex ideas into manageable steps and explaining them clearly. Lessons can be used to work through a difficult topic, improve problem-solving skills, prepare for assessments or develop stronger exam and revision techniques.

Her aim is not simply to help students complete a particular question. She encourages students to ask questions, think through problems themselves and gradually become more confident and independent learners.

Academic research experience

Alongside her medical studies and tutoring, Zainab has been involved in academic research.

Her research work has contributed to articles published in international peer-reviewed journals, giving her additional experience communicating scientific concepts accurately and clearly.

Teaching approach

Friendly, structured and personalised lessons

Zainab adapts lessons according to where each student is struggling and what they need to achieve.

01

Identify the difficulty

Zainab first identifies where understanding is breaking down rather than simply repeating the same explanation or method.

02

Simplify the concept

Difficult ideas are broken into smaller steps and explained clearly so the student can build understanding gradually.

03

Practise independently

Students are encouraged to work through problems themselves, ask questions and apply what they have learnt rather than relying on constant prompting.

04

Prepare for assessment

Exam and revision techniques can be incorporated into lessons so students learn how to apply their knowledge effectively under assessment conditions.
