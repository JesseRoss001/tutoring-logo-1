About Arianwen

Making Science Feel More Manageable

Arianwen provides supportive one-to-one tuition for students studying Biology and Chemistry at KS3 and GCSE.

She is patient, approachable and particularly enjoys breaking difficult topics into simpler, clearer explanations so that students can build their understanding gradually.

Arianwen has a strong understanding of WJEC GCSE Biology and Chemistry and has studied the WJEC exam board herself. This gives her useful first-hand familiarity with the course and some of the challenges students may experience.

First-Hand Familiarity with WJEC

Arianwen studied the WJEC exam board herself and has a strong understanding of WJEC GCSE Biology and Chemistry.

Keep Difficult Topics Simple

Arianwen's main strength is taking scientific content that initially feels complicated and breaking it down into clearer, more manageable explanations.
