Digital whiteboard lessons

A complete lesson record with resources in one place

Andy uses a digital whiteboard throughout his online lessons. The completed whiteboard can be saved for the student, providing a full record of explanations, examples and work covered.

Students can annotate the shared whiteboard during the lesson, allowing them to participate actively rather than simply watching an explanation.

Notes, videos, handouts, questions and mark schemes can be placed directly onto the whiteboard before the lesson, helping each session remain organised and focused.

Whiteboard benefits

  • A saved record of lesson notes and examples
  • Student annotation and active participation
  • Resources prepared directly on the lesson board
  • Questions and mark schemes available during the lesson
  • Easier review and revision after the session
