GROUP TUTORING FAQ

Questions About Group Tuition

Students join a live online lesson in a small group with learners working at a similar level or towards similar goals. Lessons can include tutor explanations, guided practice, questions and exam preparation.

Our Year 6 SATs groups are planned for a maximum of five pupils. Private GCSE and A-Level groups can usually be formed with two to five students.

Year 6 SATs group tuition costs £15 per student for a one-hour online lesson. GCSE and A-Level group prices depend on the tutor, subject and number of students in the group.

SATs English groups cover Reading comprehension and Grammar, Punctuation and Spelling. SATs Maths groups focus on Arithmetic, Reasoning and problem solving.

Prefer One-to-One Tuition?

Group tuition is not the right fit for every learner. If you would prefer individual support, tell us the student's subject, year group, exam board, preferred times and tutor preferences and we can help find a suitable one-to-one tutor.
