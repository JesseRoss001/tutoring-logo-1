About Donna

More than 25 years supporting Primary learners

Donna is a qualified teacher with more than 25 years of classroom experience. Her career includes teaching in both mainstream schools and the private sector, as well as several years of online teaching.

She has taught across the full Primary age range, from Nursery through to Year 6. Her particular strength is Key Stage 1, and her most recent classroom experience included teaching a combined Reception, Year 1 and Year 2 class.

Donna specialises in Primary English and Maths, with extensive experience teaching phonics, reading, comprehension and creative writing.

She has also taught online for five years, including teaching Year 1 within an online school, and has experience delivering private tuition both one-to-one and in groups.

KS1 specialist experience

Donna's strongest area is supporting younger Primary pupils during the crucial early years when confidence, literacy and numeracy foundations are being established.

Areas Donna can support

Donna's Primary tuition can focus on individual difficulties, classroom learning, building confidence or strengthening important foundations before children move onto more advanced work.

Primary Maths

Primary English

Personalised literacy support to strengthen reading, writing, vocabulary and communication skills.

Phonics

Reading

Writing

Teaching approach

Experienced Primary teaching with strong foundations first

Donna's experience across different Primary ages allows her to identify the foundational skills a child needs and build learning progressively from there.

Age appropriate

Activities and explanations are matched to the child's age, stage of development and current understanding.

Strong foundations

Missing literacy or numeracy foundations are addressed before pupils are expected to tackle more difficult work.

Confidence building

Children are supported at an appropriate pace so that they can ask questions and build confidence alongside academic understanding.

Progressive learning

Lessons develop skills step by step so children can increasingly apply what they have learned more independently.
