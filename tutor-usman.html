About Usman

Experienced teaching shaped by more than 16 years in education

Usman is a reflective practitioner with more than 16 years of teaching and tutoring experience in international and multicultural educational settings.

During his career, he has delivered Cambridge IGCSE and A-Level curricula and worked with students across a wide range of ability levels through both one-to-one and group tuition.

His approach combines strong subject knowledge with effective pedagogical strategies, differentiated instruction, regular assessment and personalised feedback.

He aims to help students move beyond simply remembering information towards developing secure conceptual understanding and the confidence to apply what they know.
