Teaching that changes with the learner

Madeleine takes time to understand each student's individual needs, goals and abilities so that her instruction can be tailored and consistent.

Her experience across languages, Primary intervention, entrance examinations and different educational settings has strengthened her belief that strong subject knowledge must be paired with an understanding of how the individual pupil learns.
