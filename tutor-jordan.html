About Jordan

Strong subject knowledge with recent first-hand exam experience

Jordan is currently studying Biomedical Engineering at Imperial College London after completing A-Levels in Further Mathematics, Mathematics and Physics with A*A*A overall.

Jordan has one year of tutoring experience across both online and in-person lessons and has worked with students ranging from Year 4 through to Year 13. This experience has provided insight into the different challenges students can face at different stages of their education.

Lessons are planned around each student's individual needs rather than following one fixed teaching style. Jordan adapts explanations, pace and practice depending on the learner's ability, confidence and response to different methods.

The aim is to help students develop genuine understanding so that they can approach unfamiliar questions more confidently rather than relying only on memorised procedures.

How Jordan teaches

Lessons built around what each student actually needs

Jordan creates tailored lesson plans and adjusts teaching methods according to the student's ability, learning style and current areas of difficulty.

Identify the priority

Lessons begin with a clear understanding of the topics the student finds difficult, what they are currently studying and where their knowledge needs strengthening.

Adapt the teaching

Jordan changes explanations, examples and lesson pace depending on how the individual student responds and what helps them understand most effectively.

Apply the concept

Students work through suitable questions so that they practise using the idea themselves rather than only watching an explanation.

Revisit and retain

Previous topics are revisited regularly so that knowledge remains secure as the student progresses through newer material.

8 weeks between regular progress checks

Jordan has used mock-style assessments approximately every eight weeks to check how well students have retained previously taught material alongside topics currently being covered at school.

Progress & assessment

Checking that knowledge actually sticks

Jordan regularly uses assessment to understand whether a student can still apply earlier learning after time has passed. This helps identify gaps that might otherwise remain unnoticed until a larger school assessment or exam.

Mock-style questions can cover both material taught during tutoring and topics currently being studied in school, giving a clearer picture of where future lessons should focus.

Tutor strengths

Clear explanations, patience and regular feedback

Jordan aims to create lessons where students feel comfortable asking questions while still being challenged to think independently and demonstrate genuine understanding.

Adaptable

Lesson structure and explanations are adjusted to reflect the individual student's needs rather than forcing every learner through the same approach.

Understanding focused

Jordan encourages students to understand why a method works so they are better prepared for unfamiliar questions and more demanding problems.

Patient & approachable

Students are encouraged to ask questions whenever something is unclear, helping them become more comfortable discussing difficulties and building confidence.

Regularly assessed

Progress is checked regularly so that weaker areas can be addressed and earlier knowledge can be reinforced before important assessments.
