About Katy

Experienced support for learners of different ages and abilities

Katy has been teaching and tutoring for ten years. During this time, she has worked with students in the United Kingdom, Spain, the United Arab Emirates and Qatar.

Her experience includes one-to-one tuition, small groups, online lessons and face-to-face teaching. The youngest learner Katy has supported was three years old, while the oldest learners have been in their fifties.

Katy teaches Primary, KS3, GCSE and A-Level learners. She also supports students completing Functional Skills English, adults learning English as a second language and homeschooled learners.

She is passionate about education and enjoys helping learners recognise their strengths, become more confident and work towards their individual goals.
