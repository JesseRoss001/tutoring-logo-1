Teaching approach

Interactive, questioning-based and discussion-led lessons

Hamza encourages students to participate actively rather than simply listen to explanations. His lessons aim to develop deeper understanding, stronger reasoning and greater independence.

Structured questioning

Hamza uses carefully chosen questions to challenge students, check understanding and encourage them to explain their reasoning.

Discussion-led learning

Students are encouraged to participate actively in lessons, discuss scientific ideas and develop independent thinking.

Handwritten notes

Hamza provides clear handwritten notes and step-by-step explanations to make difficult concepts easier to follow.

Exam-focused practice

GCSE and A-Level exam-style questions and past papers are used to develop technique, confidence and familiarity with assessment.

Between lessons

Homework and personalised feedback after every session

Hamza extends learning beyond the lesson itself by providing follow-up work and personalised feedback.

This gives students an opportunity to reinforce what they have covered, practise independently and identify what should be revisited next.

Homework after each session

Students receive work to help reinforce concepts covered during tutoring and maintain progress between lessons.

Personalised feedback

Feedback is tailored to the individual student, helping identify strengths, misconceptions and priorities for future sessions.

Exam preparation

Building the skills needed to apply knowledge under exam conditions

Hamza places strong emphasis on exam-style practice so students learn how to use their knowledge rather than only recall it.

Past papers and targeted questioning can also highlight misconceptions early, allowing future sessions to focus on the areas where the student can make the greatest progress.
