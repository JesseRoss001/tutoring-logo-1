About Siôn

Making difficult Maths clearer and more manageable

Siôn has tutored for more than 11 years and has delivered approximately 3,400 hours of one-to-one tuition to students with a wide range of abilities and confidence levels.

His approach centres on making difficult mathematical ideas accessible. Rather than asking students simply to memorise a method, Siôn breaks problems into manageable stages and helps learners understand why each step works.

Lessons are adapted to the individual student. Siôn changes the pace, level of questioning and amount of practice depending on the learner's current understanding, confidence and areas of difficulty.

He is patient, approachable and focused on helping students become increasingly independent so that they can tackle questions with greater confidence both in lessons and on their own.

Welsh-medium Maths experience

A substantial part of Siôn's tutoring experience has been teaching GCSE Mathematics through Welsh medium.

This makes him particularly well suited to students in Welsh-medium education who want mathematical support that reflects the terminology and learning environment they are familiar with at school.

Experience

Siôn has worked with students across a broad range of ability levels, from learners who lack confidence in the basics to students aiming to strengthen performance in more demanding Maths courses.

Over those years, he has frequently helped students reach their target grades and, in many cases, progress beyond them. Many of the students he has supported have improved by at least one grade.

Teaching approach

Identify the gap, explain it clearly, then build independence

Siôn uses targeted questioning and carefully selected practice to understand exactly where a student is struggling before deciding what needs to happen next.

Identify gaps

Targeted questions are used to find the precise point where understanding starts to weaken rather than assuming the whole topic needs reteaching.

Break it down

More difficult problems are separated into manageable stages, allowing the student to understand each part before putting the full method together.

Targeted practice

Practice is chosen around the student's weaker areas so lesson time is focused where it can make the greatest difference.

Build independence

Support is gradually reduced as understanding improves, helping students become more confident solving unfamiliar questions for themselves.

Confidence & progress

Building understanding that lasts

Siôn's aim is for students to leave lessons understanding the Mathematics more deeply, not simply remembering a sequence of steps until the next test.

Genuine understanding

Students are encouraged to understand why methods work, making it easier to apply their knowledge when a question is presented differently.

Confidence

A patient and approachable teaching style gives students space to ask questions, make mistakes and improve without feeling rushed.

Lasting progress

Lessons focus on strengthening weaker areas and developing the skills students need to continue progressing independently.
