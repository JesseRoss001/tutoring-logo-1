SATs papers should diagnose, not just test

Searches for Year 6 SATs papers, maths SATs papers and KS2 practice papers are extremely common. Papers are valuable, but repeating them without reviewing the causes of mistakes can create frustration rather than progress.

Before the paper: teach the knowledge and methods your child needs.
During the paper: practise timing, careful reading and sensible checking.
After the paper: group mistakes by topic and turn them into a revision plan.

Understanding SATs results

What does a scaled score of 100 mean?

In the English Key Stage 2 national curriculum tests, a scaled score of 100 or more means a pupil has met the expected standard in that test. Scores are reported on a scale from 80 to 120. Our focus is not on chasing a number through pressure. It is on developing the underlying maths, reading and English skills that allow pupils to perform confidently.
