About Sarah

Three decades of experience supporting learners

Sarah has 30 years of teaching and tutoring experience. Since leaving classroom teaching, she has continued to develop her skills through face-to-face, online, one-to-one and group teaching in her specialist subject areas.

She supports Primary and KS3 learners, as well as students preparing for GCSE and IGCSE English. Sarah also teaches ESOL and Music.

Her experience enables her to recognise individual strengths, identify areas that require support and adapt lesson content to suit each student.

Sarah aims to create lessons that are engaging, focused and purposeful while helping learners become more confident and independent.

“I aim to relate to, engage and encourage students through interesting, varied and focused activities so that they can achieve confidence and success.”

Sarah
