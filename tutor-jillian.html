About Jillian

A Friendly Environment Where Learning Stays the Focus

Jillian has 13 years of tutoring experience and has delivered both face-to-face and online lessons to learners across a range of ages.

Her approach is highly personalised. She wants each student to feel more relaxed and confident during tuition while still making sure the lesson remains purposeful and connected to what they need academically.

Jillian places particular importance on developing a good rapport with the learner, as well as maintaining positive communication with parents or guardians where appropriate.

She believes a friendly but learning-focused environment helps students participate more confidently and makes each one-to-one session more productive.

Personalised, but curriculum connected

Jillian adapts lessons to the individual learner while keeping the work relevant to their curriculum, current learning and academic goals.
