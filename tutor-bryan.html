About Bryan

Supportive language tuition for learners of different ages and backgrounds

Bryan is a TEFL-certified English and Welsh tutor with over 12 years of teaching experience. He has supported children, teenagers, adults and professionals at a wide range of ability levels.

His experience includes one-to-one online lessons, classroom teaching, conversational English, pronunciation work, fluency development and exam preparation support.

Bryan has taught in the United Kingdom, South Korea and Italy, as well as online with learners from many different countries and cultural backgrounds.

He adapts each lesson to the student’s age, current ability, confidence and individual goals. His aim is to help learners feel comfortable using English or Welsh naturally while becoming more confident, motivated and independent.
