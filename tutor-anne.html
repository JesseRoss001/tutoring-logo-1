About Anne

Extensive classroom experience brought into personalised online lessons

Anne has more than 30 years of experience teaching Science in the classroom. During her teaching career, she worked as a Head of Science, Head of Year and examiner, giving her a broad understanding of both subject teaching and student development.

After working as a full-time Science teacher, Anne moved into tutoring so that she could provide more focused support to individual students.

She works with learners across Biology, Chemistry, Physics and Combined Science at KS3, GCSE and A-Level. Her experience allows her to identify areas of difficulty, address gaps in understanding and help students approach their studies with greater confidence.

Anne's approach

“I discuss the needs of the student and adapt each lesson to their individual needs.”
  • Lessons personalised to the learner's current understanding and goals
  • Specific areas of difficulty identified and addressed
  • Explanations and lesson pace adapted to the student
  • A strong focus on building confidence and independent understanding
